Streaming World-Action Models for Robotic Manipulation

StreamWAM is a research framework and unified testbed for systematically studying streaming strategies in world-action-model-based robot control.

Our action-conditioned streaming formulation feeds the prefix of actions currently being executed back into the world model. The executed actions shape the predicted visual future while StreamWAM asynchronously infers the next world-action chunk.

The core idea

Execution becomes part of prediction.

StreamWAM couples inference and execution at the modeling level. It does not treat their overlap as a systems-only optimization: the actions already in motion become context for what the world model predicts next.

Unified testbed

Compare streaming strategies on equal ground.

StreamWAM provides a common framework for studying how different streaming choices affect both task success and control latency in WAM-based manipulation.

Action conditioned

Close the loop between what acts and what imagines.

The action prefix is not merely scheduled alongside inference. It directly conditions the predicted visual future, connecting asynchronous generation to ongoing execution.
